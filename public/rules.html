Rules of engagement

Flag format

Every flag follows the pattern Eclipse{...}. In the flag terminal you only type what goes inside the braces; the Eclipse{ and } are added for you. Pasting a full Eclipse{...} string also works.

Scoring

Each case awards fixed points on your first correct flag. A case can only be scored once. Your rank is decided by total score, and ties are broken by whoever reached that score first.

Intel (hints)

Each case has intel you can decrypt. Every piece of intel costs a fixed number of points, shown before you unlock it. Intel is progressive — you must decrypt earlier intel first. The cost is charged the moment you decrypt it, whether or not you later solve the case, so your score can go below zero.

Submission limit

You may transmit up to 5 flag attempts every 60 seconds, counted across all cases. Wrong guesses are logged. Automating brute-force submissions is not permitted.

Accessing the live target (WEB-03)

One case runs a live, intentionally vulnerable web service behind Cloudflare Access. To reach it:

  • Browser / Burp: open the access link, enter your allowed email, and type the one-time PIN emailed to you. In Burp, sign in through the embedded browser.
  • Command line: run cloudflared access login <url> once, then use cloudflared access curl <url>. Alternatively copy the CF_Authorization cookie from the browser into curl -b.

If the primary link is down, check the case page for a backup link activated by Sentinel Control.

Code of conduct

Attack only the challenge targets provided. Do not attack the scoring platform, its infrastructure, other responders, or any system outside the challenge scope. Do not share flags. Play fair.
